How it works

  1. Join once. The event's floors download to your phone, so everything below works with the venue's network down.
  2. Say where you are. Something near you: "I'm at the elevators", "registration", a room's name. No camera, no map to read.
  3. Ask the way. "How do I get to room 204?", "Where's booth 42?", "Take me to the coffee". You're told the way in words, one stretch at a time: "Walk straight about 12 meters, past the coffee station on your right, then turn left." Say next when you're ready for the next one, repeat to hear it again, and I'm there when you arrive.
  4. Camera if you want it. For the last few meters, Guide me with the camera steers you to the exact spot, as Whereabouts does at home.

Finding works on any iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Whereabouts speaks English and Arabic.

Good to know

  • The map was made by the organizers. Things move at events: when something isn't where you were told, say that's not there and the organizers hear about it.
  • Directions describe the floor as it was scanned. They don't see people or things moved into the way; use them with your cane or guide dog, as you would any directions.
